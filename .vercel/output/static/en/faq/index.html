Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I need to have previous experience to take the classes?

No, it is not necessary. My classes are designed for all levels, from absolute beginners (A1) to advanced students (C2). Before starting, I conduct a free assessment of your level to adapt the classes to your needs.

How are online classes conducted?

All classes are taught on video call platforms like Zoom or Google Meet. All you need is a good internet connection and a device with a camera and microphone (computer, tablet or smartphone). At the end of each class, you will receive support material and exercises for you to practice between sessions.

Do you offer a free trial class?

Yes! I offer a first free trial class of 30 minutes. During this session, we will assess your Spanish level and define your goals together. You can also get to know my teaching style and ask me any questions you have before starting the course.

What is the duration of each class?

Each class has a standard duration of 60 minutes, but if you prefer, I can offer shorter sessions (45 minutes) or longer sessions (90 minutes), depending on your availability and preferences. Just let me know when we schedule the classes.

What levels of Spanish do you teach?

I teach students of all levels, from beginner (A1) to advanced (C2). Here is a quick description: Beginner (A1 - A2): Learn the basics, from greetings and essential vocabulary to simple grammatical structures. Intermediate (B1 - B2): Improve your fluency, learn to hold conversations on everyday topics and start using more complex expressions. Advanced (C1 - C2): Perfect your Spanish to speak naturally in professional or academic situations, or if you plan to live in a Spanish-speaking country.

How long will it take to speak Spanish fluently?

The time you will need to speak Spanish fluently will depend on several factors, such as your current level, the frequency of the classes, and how much you practice outside of the sessions. In general, students who take classes regularly (1-2 times a week) and practice between classes usually notice a great improvement in 3 to 6 months.

Booking and Payment

How much do the classes cost?

I offer several options according to your needs: Individual 60-minute class: €25 5-class package: €110 (€22 per class) 10-class package: €200 (€20 per class) You can check all the details of the prices and plans in the [Plans and Prices] section.

Can I cancel or reschedule a class?

Of course! I understand that your schedule may change. If you need to cancel or reschedule a class, you just have to notify me at least 24 hours in advance. If you do not do so within this period, the class will be considered given.

How is the payment for classes made?

Payment can be made by bank transfer or PayPal. I will provide you with the payment details once we confirm your reservation. You can pay per class or purchase class packages at a reduced price.

How do I book a class?

You can book a class through my [Contact Form], or directly by clicking on the "Book your class" button on the main page. You can also contact me by email or WhatsApp to arrange the details and choose the schedule that best suits you.

Features

In what language are the classes taught?

My classes are in Spanish from day one, but if you need it, I can explain in French or English so that you better understand some difficult concepts. My goal is for you to feel comfortable while improving your Spanish.

Do you offer specialized classes (business, tourism, etc.)?

Yes, I offer specialized classes in Spanish for different professional areas, such as tourism, business, medicine, and more. These classes are designed to help you acquire the specific vocabulary and expressions you need in your field of work.

Can I learn Spanish for travel?

Yes, I offer a specific course for travelers who want to learn the most useful phrases and vocabulary before their trip to a Spanish-speaking country. You will learn how to communicate in restaurants, hotels, shops, and in common situations during the trip.

How can I improve my Spanish outside of classes?

In addition to the lessons, I recommend that you practice every day by listening to podcasts in Spanish, watching series or movies with subtitles, and using applications like Duolingo or Memrise. I will also provide you with a list of additional resources so that you can continue practicing and improving on your own.

Can I share my classes with a friend or family member?

If you are interested in learning with someone else, I also offer the option of classes in small groups (maximum 3 people). This option is ideal if you and a friend or family member have a similar level of Spanish and want to learn together. Contact me for more details on prices and organization.

Do you have more questions?

If you have not found the answer you were looking for, do not hesitate to contact me.
